Ethical Manufacturing

Made the Way Your Customers Expect

Shoppers increasingly want to know who made their clothes and how those people are treated. At Asha Garment Factory, that question has a good answer. Since 2011 we have made garments in Hyderabad with fair wages, fixed working hours and safe working conditions.

How It Works

From Sample to Shipment

Samples take about 3 weeks. Once you approve the sample, bulk production takes 6–10 weeks depending on quantity, fabric and embellishment.

01

Sampling

Send a tech pack, sketch or reference garment. We develop your first sample in about three weeks and revise until you approve it.

02

Fabric & Trims

We source the fabric, trims and labels your design needs from selected mills and suppliers, and confirm them with you.

03

Cutting & Stitching

Patterns are cut and sewn on industrial lockstitch and overlock machines by skilled craftspeople in our Hyderabad factory.

04

Embellishment

Embroidery, screen printing, heat transfer, labels and hangtags are planned into production and quality-checked with the garment.

05

Quality Control

Three-stage inspection — in-process, post-production and pre-shipment — covering stitching, colour fastness, measurements and finish.

06

Packing & Export

Export packing, documentation and shipping to the USA, UK, Europe, the Middle East or wherever your brand is based.
