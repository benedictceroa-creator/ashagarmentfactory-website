Private Label Manufacturing

Your Brand, Made in Our Hyderabad Factory

Asha Garment Factory has made private label and OEM clothing for brands in the USA, UK, Switzerland and the Gulf since 2011. You bring the idea — a full tech pack, a sketch, or a garment you want to improve on — and we take care of sampling, fabric, production, branding and shipping.

Every order leaves our factory carrying your brand: your neck labels, care labels, hangtags and packaging. One team manages the whole process, so you have a single point of contact from first sample to final shipment.

  • Your labels, hangtags and custom packaging
  • Minimum order from 100 pieces per style
  • Sample in about 3 weeks; bulk in 6–10 weeks after approval
  • Export packing and documentation handled for you
