Ethical Manufacturing

Made the Way Your Customers Expect

Shoppers increasingly want to know who made their clothes and how those people are treated. At Asha Garment Factory, that question has a good answer. Since 2011 we have made garments in Hyderabad with fair wages, fixed working hours and safe working conditions.

And our purpose goes further than the factory floor: our profits fund a mission to educate underprivileged children and help single mothers build skills and independence.

  • Fair wages and fixed working hours
  • Safe working conditions
  • Fabric waste minimisation in cutting and production
  • Profits support children's education and single mothers
  • Chosen by ethical fashion brands in the USA
