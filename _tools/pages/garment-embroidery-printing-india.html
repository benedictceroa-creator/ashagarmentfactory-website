Embellishment

Decoration Built Into Production

Embroidery and prints are often what make a garment recognisably yours. At Asha Garment Factory, embellishment is planned into your order from the first sample, not added as an afterthought — and the same team quality-checks the decoration, the stitching and the finish together.

Our in-house multi-head embroidery machines handle logos, monograms and decorative motifs, and we offer screen printing and heat transfer for graphics and branding.

  • In-house multi-head machine embroidery
  • Screen printing and heat transfer
  • Placement, size and colours confirmed in the sample
  • Custom woven or printed labels and hangtags
  • From 100 pieces per style
